ABOUT THE PROJECT

Travel planning for every budget.

Smart Travel Planner is a tourism project concept that helps travellers discover Indian destinations, compare practical budget tiers, and prepare a day-wise itinerary.

All included destination prices are sample estimates for demonstration, not real-time fare or hotel quotes. Verify current prices, opening hours, permits and availability before travelling.
